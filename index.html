The Operator

A career built on building category leaders.

Robbert Rietbroek is President and CEO of Graphic Packaging Holding Company, the publicly traded paper-based packaging leader trading on the NYSE under the ticker GPK. He took the top role after a quarter-century inside some of the most demanding consumer-products organizations on earth — Procter & Gamble, Kimberly-Clark, PepsiCo, and most recently Primo Brands, where he served as inaugural CEO of the publicly traded bottled-water company formed by the 2024 merger of Primo Water and BlueTriton Brands.

The thread connecting those roles is unusually consistent: take a brand or business unit, sharpen its strategy, raise its operating standard, and grow it. Rietbroek has done that across Europe, South America, Australia, New Zealand, and the United States — first in marketing seats, then as a general manager, then as a CEO. His full career arc and current focus at Graphic Packaging sit on the about page.

Outside the day job, he serves on the boards of the Metro Atlanta Chamber and the American Forest and Paper Association, sits on the Wall Street Journal CEO Council, and holds three packaging-design patents.
